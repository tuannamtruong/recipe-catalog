## Ingredients
- 5 brocolli/cauliflower heads
- 200g beans/chickpeas
- olive oil
- 1 taco
- Sauce:
- 2 tsps maple syrup
- 3tbs hot sauce
- little lime

## Steps
1. Med heat + olive oil + bean + veggie. Cook until soft.
2. Add the sauce.
3. Mashes.
4. Make a taco.
5. 200 C 10 mins

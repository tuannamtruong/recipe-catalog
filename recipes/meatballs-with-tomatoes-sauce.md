## Ingredients
- 500g thịt băm
- 4 small onions
- 6 cloves of garlics
- 200g can tomatoes
- 500g tomatoes
- 1/2 tbs tomatoes puree
- 1 tbsp (sherry) vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- Basil
- Thyme
- Rosmarin

## Steps
1. In a bowl: 1 (cooked) onion, 2 (cooked) garlic, rosamrin, thyme, meat, salt, pepper.
2. Make meatballs. Chill the balls for a while.
3. In a stove, low heat: oil, 3 small chopped onions, 4 whole garlic, closed lit for 10-15 mins.
4. Add chopped tomatoes, can tomatoes, vinegar, sugar. Cook for 40 mins, medium heat, stir.
5. Add basil to the sauce
6. Cook the meatballs (in different pan or oven 200C), then throw in the sauce for 10 mins.

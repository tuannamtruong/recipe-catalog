## Ingredients
- 115g butter, melted
- 250g white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbs vanilla extract
- 25g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 60g all-purpose flour

## Steps
1. Butter melted then in a tray
2. Sugar in then mix
3. Egg in then mix
4. Vanilla, koko powder, salt, flour together and mix
5. 180C for 20 mins
6. Vegan

## Ingredients
- 800g Pork loin
- 4 cloves of garlic
- Ginger
- 1/3 cup Soy sauce
- 1/4 cup Sesame oil
- 2 tbs Worchester sauce
- 1/8 cup brown sugar
- 3 green onions
- 1 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 1/2 tbs chili paste

## Steps
1. Mix: soy sauce, sesame oil, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, green onions, garlic, chile paste, and pepper.
2. Tenderloin in 8 hours at least.
3. Preheat oven 230C
4. Pork in alu folie
5. Roast 25-30 mins.
6. 5 min rest.

## Ingredients
- 500g pork loin
- Salt and black pepper
- BBQ SAUCE
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/4 cup finely diced onion
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1 tablespoon hot chili sauce
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar (any sour liquid)
- Salt and ground pepper, to taste

## Steps
1. Oven to 135C.
2. Salt and pepper. Cover with alu foil. Bake meat size up for 3 hrs.
3. Oil in pan over med heat. Add onions and cook until soft and translucent. Add cumin and cook for 30s. Add ketchup, chili sauce, sugar, and apple cider vinegar. Stir to combine, season with salt then cook for 2 minutes.
4. Finish with broil the chops with sauce on both side in oven. Or cook in the pan for a while.

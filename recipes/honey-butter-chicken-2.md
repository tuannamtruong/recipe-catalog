## Ingredients
- 1kg chicken breast/tofu
- 1 cup (240 ml) cornstarch
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Sauce:
- 10 tbs honey
- +2 tbs sugar
- 5 tbs soy sauce
- 3 tbs water
- 2 inch piece ginger, grated
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter
- Sesame seed for garnish

## Steps
1. Cut into strips
2. Coat protein with Cornstarch, salt, pepper and garlic powder.
3. Airfryer: 190C 18 mins.
4. Combine sauce and mix.
5. Med-low heat with butter.
6. Sauce in.
7. Reduce.
8. Combine with protein.
9. Garnish.

## Ingredients
- 500g chicken breast
- Oil
- 1 medium onion
- 2 tablespoons butter divided
- 3 garlic cloves
- 200g mushroom
- 470ml heavy cream

## Steps
1. Season with salt, pepper
2. Medium-high heat.
3. Brown chicken.
4. Remove meat.
5. Med heat.
6. Oil and butter.
7. Cook onion until soft.
8. Add mushroom and cook until brownish.
9. Add garlic and cook until fragant.
10. Stir in the cream.
11. Add salt and pepper (and sth sweet).
12. Cook until thicken.

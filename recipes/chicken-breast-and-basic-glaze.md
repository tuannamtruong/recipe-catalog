## Ingredients
- 1 chicken breast
- Salt and Pepper to taste
- 1tsp garlic
- Juice/red wine
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 2 tbs brown sugar

## Steps
1. Season chicken with salt, peper, and garlic.
2. Brown both side
3. Juice in pan with paprika and sugar.
4. Simmer low heat for 10 mins.

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups chicken broth
- 1 beef bouillon cube
- 1 tsp low sodium soy sauce, can sub Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp thyme,
- ½ tsp mustard powder
- 3 tbs butter
- 10 whole cloves garlic
- 3 tbs flour
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese, (optional)

## Steps
1. Broth mixture: chicken broth, bouillon cube, soy sauce, mustard powder, onion powder.
2. Melt the butter in the skillet med-low heat.
3. Add the garlic cloves and let them soften and caramelize for 6-7 minutes.
4. Add the flour and cook for 1-2 minutes.
5. Add the broth mixture in splashes, stirring continuously.
6. Stir in the cream. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low.
7. Add thyme, cheese.
8. Add the protein back to the skillet.
9. Cover partially and simmer for 5 minutes.
10. Garnish with parsley.
11. MISC

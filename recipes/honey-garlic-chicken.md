## Ingredients
- 500g thịt gà
- salt and pepper
- 1 cup flour
- 3,5 tbs unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1,5 apple vinegar
- 1tbs soy sauce (oyster sauce)
- 1/3 cup honey

## Steps
1. Breats medium cut
2. salt and pepper.
3. Coat chicken in flour
4. Melt 3,3 tbs butter in a large skillet over high heat.
5. cook chicken for 2 – 3 minutes until golden. Turn and cook the other side for 1 minute.
6. Heat down to medium high.
7. Make room in the pan, add garlic and top with 0,2 tbs of butter. Stir garlic briefly once butter melts.
8. Add vinegar, soy sauce and honey. Stir / shake pan to combine.
9. Bring sauce to simmer, then simmer for 1 minute or until slightly thickened.
10. Turn chicken to coat in sauce. If the sauce gets too thick, add a touch of water and stir.

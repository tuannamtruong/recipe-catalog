## Ingredients
- 250g steak
- Oil
- Salt, pepper
- 1tbs Butter
- 250g mushroom
- 1 medium Onion
- 2 tbs Hot paprika
- Thyme
- 1 Cup dry white wine
- 2 cup chicken broth
- 200g Pasta
- worcestershire sauce
- 200g sour cream

## Steps
1. Brown steak both side, move and cover in alu foil.
2. Mushroom: Quartered
3. Butter in till foamed
4. Cook mushroom with salt till brown
5. Lower heat, add onion cook till brown
6. Add paprika and thyme for aprox 30s
7. Add dry white wine and chicken broth
8. Cook to rolling simmer, then add pasta
9. When liquid down 90%, heat to low. Add a dash of worcestershire sauce and cream.
10. Finish with parsley, salt and pepper.
11. Cut steak. Move in to pan. Cover pan. Remove heat for 5 mins.

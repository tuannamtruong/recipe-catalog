## Ingredients
- 400g chickpeas
- 200g cooked quinoa
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1/3 cup mint, chopped
- 1/3 cup parsley, chopped
- 1/2 cup pistachio, crumbled pecan
- 1/2 cup Lemon vinaigrette:
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ honey, or maple syrup
- ¼ to ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ ¼ thyme, optional

## Steps
1. 3 medium apples
2. Chop in small pieces then sauté in a chunk of butter.
3. 2 Tbsp sugar
4. a good dash of cinnamon
5. Mix and cook on low heat for 5-10mins.
6. Crumbles:
7. 110g flour
8. 90g soft vegan butter or margarine
9. 70g sugar or brown sugar
10. a pinch of salt
11. Mix to get a crumbly mix.
12. Evenly distribute over the apples (if your pan isn’t oven proof, use a baking dish).
13. Bake at 180C for 30-35mins.
14. 45min

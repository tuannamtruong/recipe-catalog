## Ingredients
- 300g tater tot’s
- 500g grounded beef
- 200g cheddar
- 3 peppers diced
- 10 eggs
- 6-10 wraps
- ketchup

## Steps
1. Air fry/Oven taters
2. Cook beef with pepper, then eggs
3. Wrap it all up with ketchup

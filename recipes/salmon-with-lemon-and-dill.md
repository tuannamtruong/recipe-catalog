## Ingredients
- 400 g salmon fillets
- ¼ cup butter, melted
- 5 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon dried dill weed
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- salt, pepper to taste

## Steps
1. Oven 175C.
2. Lightly grease a medium baking dish.
3. All ingredients together in the dish
4. Bake for 25 mins.

## Ingredients
- pasta of choice
- 1 cucumber
- 1 bell pepper
- 1 red onion
- 300 g chickpease
- some Kalamata olive
- 100g Feta cheese
- 300g halved cherry tomatoes
- Seasoning:
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder, italian seasoning
- Dressing:
- hummus
- lime juice
- salt
- water to thin

## Steps
1. Cook pasta
2. Chop veggies, mix together.
3. Seasoning
4. Makes dressing when serve.

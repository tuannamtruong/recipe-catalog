## Ingredients
- 1 cucumber
- Cut in half, smash and cut in slices
- 1 clove garlic
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 1 Tbsp tahini
- 3/4 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup or sugar
- 1 Tsp (rice) vinegar
- chili flakes and salt to taste
- (tomatoes, bellpepper, nuts)

## Steps
1. Shake well and enjoy.

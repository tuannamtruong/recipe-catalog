## Ingredients
- 500g chicken breast
- Roux:
- Butter
- Flour
- 1 tbs Curry
- 1 tsp Garam Masala
- Curry:
- 3 cloves Garlic
- 1 shallot
- Soy sauce
- Apple juice/vinegar
- Honey (sugar)
- Veggie:
- 3 carrots
- 3 potatoes

## Steps
1. Cắt cà rốt, khoai tây biting size. Chuẩn bị chicken broth.
2. Nấu Roux: butter,  flour, Curry, Garam masala. Transfer to a bowl.
3. Lửa 7/10, oil, minced garlic, shallots, salt, roux.
4. Add: chicken, soy sauce, honey, sth chua(juice/vinegar), chicken broth,  veggie
5. Simmer 45 mins.

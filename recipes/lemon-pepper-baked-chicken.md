## Ingredients
- 500g chicken breast (halved)
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbs butter
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- MIX:
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1 tsp (lemon) Pepper
- 1/2 cup flour
- zest of 1 lemon

## Steps
1. Combine MIX.
2. Coat chicken.
3. Slice the lemon into slices.
4. Cook chicken in oil until brown on both side.
5. To skillet: butter, garlic, and lemon slices.
6. Broth to skillet.
7. Cook until chicken is cooked through and sauce has reduced slightly (3 minutes).

## Ingredients
- Protein:
- 1kg chicken/tofu
- 240 ml cornstarch
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- Sauce:
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter
- 8 tbsp honey
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 inch piece ginger, grated
- Garnish:
- Sesame seed

## Steps
1. Cornstarch + protein + garlic + pepper. Coat evenly
2. 190 for 17 mins in air fry
3. Mix honey + soy sauce + grated ginger.
4. Cook with butter in a pan, low heat. Reduce.
5. Add protein and cook.
6. Add garnish.

## Ingredients
- 1 Pork loin (500g)
- 2 Shallots /1 med onion
- 4 Garlic cloves
- 40g Butter
- 2 canned chipotle peppers
- 1 tsp peanut butter
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp (at most) ground cloves
- 3/4 tsp flour
- 1 tsp tomato paste
- 1-2 tbs any fruit preserves

## Steps
1. Remove the connect tissue
2. Salt and pepper the loin
3. Sear in med-high heat. Move constantly.
4. Heat to med-low. Add garlic, shallots, and butter.
5. Cook meat for around 15 mins. 63C is good.
6. Add chitpole pepper (or sth similar) and peanut butter
7. Add some cocoa powder.
8. Add cinnamon, cumin, oregano, cloves
9. Add pinch of flour
10. Tomato paste
11. Degalze with broth or water
12. Reduce to thick sauce
13. Add some preserved fruit or dried fruit.

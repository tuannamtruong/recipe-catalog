## Ingredients
- Shallot
- Garlic
- Tomato paste
- (Can) Tomatoe

## Steps
1. Fry shallot, garlic and tomato paste. Salt, pepper
2. Add (can) crushed tomatoe and a glug of white wine.
3. Stir often

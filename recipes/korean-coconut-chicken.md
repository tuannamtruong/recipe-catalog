## Ingredients
- Marinate:
- 1kg chicken thighs
- Salt and pepper
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp gochujang
- olive oil
- Drizzle of honey
- For the Sauce:
- 3 small onions, diced
- 2 carrot, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tsp grated ginger
- 4 tsp gochujang (to taste)
- 1L coconut milk
- 2,5 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbs brown sugar

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken.
2. Golden the chicken in both side.
3. Put the chicken aside.
4. Cook onion and carrot for 3–4 min until slightly softened.
5. Add garlic, ginger, and gochujang and cook until fragrant.
6. Pour in the coconut milk, soy sauce and brown sugar. Let it simmer gently until slightly thickened.
7. Slice the chicken and back to the sauce.

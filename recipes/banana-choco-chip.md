## Ingredients
- 3 bananas (mashed)
- 115 g butter
- 150g brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp vanilla
- 120 ml of buttermilk
- 120 ml greek yogurt or sour cream
- 250g flour
- 1 tsp of baking soda pinch of salt
- 1 cup of chocolate chips

## Steps
1. Chill butter
2. Preheat oven 175C
3. save some choco chips for topping
4. banana mashed + chilled butter + egg + sugar + yogurt + flour + choco chip
5. Mix little
6. bake at 175 C for about 45-50 minutes

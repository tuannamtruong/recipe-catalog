## Ingredients
- 400g sausage
- 1/2 yellow onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 400g roasted tomatoes
- 2 garlic cloves
- 475–710 ml beef broth
- 225g pasta
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 85g cream cheese
- 400g any beans
- For the Cajun Seasoning:
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Brown sausage and remove from pan
2. Onion, pepper + cajun seasoning + garlic
3. 30s
4. 400g tomatoes + beef broth + pasta + heavy cream + beans

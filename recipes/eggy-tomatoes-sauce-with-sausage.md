## Ingredients
- 2 tomatoes
- 2 eggs
- 200g sausages
- 2 tbs minced garlic
- sausage
- 1/2 cup (119 ml) water
- 2 tbs ketchup

## Steps
1. Cut tomatoes to small chunk
2. Make the egg
3. Make the sausage
4. Add garlic, tomatoes and water
5. Cook tomatoes until all juice get out
6. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, ketchup
7. Add egg and sausage

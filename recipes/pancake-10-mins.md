## Ingredients
- 1 egg
- 50g greek yogurt
- 35g flour (oat flour?)
- 1tsp baking powder
- 30g protein powder
- 80ml milk
- sweetener of choice
- topping:
- Choco
- berries and almond
- banana and nut

## Steps
1. Mix everything until smooth.
2. Put inside an oven 175C for 30 mins.

## Ingredients
- 10 Garlics
- 2 onions
- 200g sun-dried tomatoes
- 1tbs tomato paste
- Mixed seasoning.
- 1tsp Cumin
- 1tsp Turmeric
- 2 cans beans
- 1 can coconut milk
- spinach
- 1 Lemon
- Nutritional yeast

## Steps
1. Cook garlic + onion + dried tomatoes + tomatoes paste with salt + cumin + turmeric.
2. Add beans, coconut milk.
3. Bubbling
4. Spinach + lemon

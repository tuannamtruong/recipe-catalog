## Ingredients
- 500g thịt băm
- 1 egg
- ⅔ cup milk
- 2 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 3 slices bread, crumbled
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
- ½ cup shredded carrot
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup ketchup
- 1 tbs yellow mustard

## Steps
1. Preheat oven 175 C
2. In a bowl: egg, milk, salt, and ground black pepper, crumbled bread and stir until dissolved.
3. Mix ground beef, onion, Cheddar, and carrot into mixture.
4. Makes a meatloaf. Ready to bake.
5. Combine brown sugar, ketchup, and mustard in a bowl. Spread over meat mixture.
6. Bake until no longer pink in the center, 1 to 1 1/4 hours.
7. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read at least 70 degrees C.
8. Rest 10 mins

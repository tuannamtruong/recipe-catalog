## Ingredients
- Burrito
- 8 burrito-size tortillas
- 500 g ground beef
- 1 cup beef broth
- 6 tsp taco seasoning (or homemade: 3 tsp chili powder, 3 tsp cumin, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tosp paprika, 1 tsp onion powder,
- 1 tbs oregano, 1 tbs salt, 1 tbs black pepper)
- 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
- (6 tostadas)
- 2 cups shredded lettuce
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 cup sour cream
- 3 tsp butter, melted
- Creamy Taco Sauce
- ½ cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup milk or water
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 1 tbsp dried parsley
- ½ tsp paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder

## Steps
1. 1. Lay the tortillas around the edges of a sheet pan so the centers sit on the lip of the pan, then place one tortilla flat in the middle.
2. Spread about ¾ cup sour cream across the tortillas.
3. 2. In a skillet, cook the ground beef with taco seasoning and beef broth until browned and the liquid is absorbed.
4. 3. Add the beef over the sour cream, then layer shredded cheese, tostadas, shredded lettuce, and diced tomatoes.
5. 4. Add a tortilla with the remaining 1/4 cup sour cream in the middle. Fold the tortillas over the top to fully enclose everything, then brush with melted butter.
6. 5. Top with another baking sheet and bake at 215C for 20 minutes. Remove the top sheet and bake for another 7 minutes until golden and crispy.
7. Sauce
8. 1. Add all ingredients to a bowl.
9. 2. Whisk until smooth and fully combined.
10. 3. Let sit for 15-30 minutes in the fridge if you have time.
11. BEEF

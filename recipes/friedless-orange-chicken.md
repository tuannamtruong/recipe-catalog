## Ingredients
- 500mg chicken
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
- 1tbs garlic, minced
- 1tbs sugar
- 2tbs orange juice
- 2tbs cup vinegar
- 1tbs soy sauce
- 0,5 tbs cornstach
- 1tbs water
- 0,5 tsb sesame oil

## Steps
1. Chicken 2x2 cubes + salt, pepper, and flour.
2. Brown chicken. Remove.
3. Pot over medium-high heat and add the oil.
4. Once the oil begins to shimmer, add ginger, garlic, and cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
5. Add sugar and stir to combine.
6. Add in the orange juice and allow the sugars to begin to dissolve in the liquid, stirring occasionally.
7. Add in the vinegar and soy sauce, and stir to combine.
8. Add the cornstarch and water together and whisk to combine. Add to the pan and stir.
9. Continue to cook the sauce until maple syrup consistency is achieved.
10. Add in the chicken and stir until completely coated in the sauce.
11. Top with sesame oil.

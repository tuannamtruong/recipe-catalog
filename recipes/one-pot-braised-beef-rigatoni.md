## Ingredients
- 1kg Chuck Roast
- Vegtables:
- 1 large Carrot diced
- 3 stalks Celery diced
- 1 large white Onion diced
- 3 cloves Garlic
- 2 tablespoons Tomato paste
- 4 cups Beef broth
- 4 cups Crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup Water
- 2 sprigs Thyme
- 2 sprigs Rosemary
- 3 Bay leaf
- 2 tbs Salt
- 1 tbs black Pepper
- 1/2 bunch Basil
- 2 tbs fresh Parsley chopped
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese

## Steps
1. Beef into large even sized chunks and season throughly with salt and pepper.
2. Medium high heat oil.
3. Sear beef on all sides then remove from pot.
4. To the same pot add your vegetables and allow to cook for 2-3 minutes while stirring.
5. Add garlic, tomato paste and cook with the vegetables for 2-3 minutes.
6. Add your broth, crushed tomato, water, thyme, rosemary and bay leaves and bring this to a simmer.
7. Add your seared beef back in and allow to simmer on low heat for 2 hours.
8. (Shread meats)

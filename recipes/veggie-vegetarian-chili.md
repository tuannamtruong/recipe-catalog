## Ingredients
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 cup chopped carrots
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 bell peppers
- 2 tbs chili powder
- 800 ml can whole peeled tomatoes
- 400 ml can black beans, drained
- 400 ml can kidney beans, drained
- 400 ml can pinto beans, drained
- 400 ml can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 ½ cups chopped fresh mushrooms
- 1 ½ tbs dried oregano
- 1 ½ tbs dried basil
- 1 tbs ground cumin
- ½ tbs garlic powder

## Steps
1. Large pot, medium heat.
2. Add onion, carrots, and garlic; cook and stir until tender.
3. Stir in bell peppers; season with chili powder. Cook until peppers are tender, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in tomatoes, black beans, kidney beans, pinto beans, corn, and mushrooms; season with oregano, basil, cumin, and garlic powder.
5. Bring to a boil.
6. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and cook 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.

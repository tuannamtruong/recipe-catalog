## Ingredients
- 400g sausage
- 1 onion finely diced
- 1 sweet potatoe finely diced
- 2 carrots finely diced
- 6 cloves garlic minced
- red pepper
- 1 teaspoon dry thyme
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 4 cups chicken/veg stock
- 400g chickpeas
- 400ml can diced tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons dry parsley
- Salt and pepper

## Steps
1. Medium heat. Cook sausage (breaking it up with a wooden spoon) until well-browned.
2. Transfer the sausage to a bowl.
3. Drain off all but 2 tablespoons of fat in the pan.
4. Med heat. add onion, sweet potato, and carrots. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Add the garlic and chili pepper to the pan and cook for 1 minute.
6. Melt the butter into the pot and add the thyme.
7. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute more until the paste deepens in color.
8. Pour the stock into the pot and scrape up any browned bits stuck to the bottom.
9. Return the sausage to the pot.
10. Add the chickpeas and tomatoes. Bring to a boil and season with salt and pepper. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

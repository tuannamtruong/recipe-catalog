## Ingredients
- Seared sth (Chicken breast, pork  chop)
- Shallot or onion
- (And) Garlic
- Wine
- Butter
- Broth/Water
- Mustard

## Steps
1. Shallots and garlic into skillet over medium heat, and cook until tender. (30s)
2. Stir in wine, and cook until heated through. (Reduced the alcohol)
3. Stir in broth/water and mustard, reduce to slightly thickened.
4. Remove skillet from heat.
5. Butter in until melted during cooldown.

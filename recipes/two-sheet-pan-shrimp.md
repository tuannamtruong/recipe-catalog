## Ingredients
- 454g shrimp (bigger ones are more likely to cook in the same time as the veggies)
- 454g asparagus/brocolli
- 2-3 shallots
- 2-3 garlic cloves
- 1 lemon
- fresh herb for garnish (lemon /parsley)

## Steps
1. Defrost shrimp
2. Everything in a pan, flat even layer. Season with oil and spices. Very little salt added.
3. broiler/grill max for 3 mins. Then flips for another 3 mins.
4. Bake

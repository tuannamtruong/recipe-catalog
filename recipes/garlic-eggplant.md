## Ingredients
- 30 mins
- 2 eggplants
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- a thumbsize piece of ginger (minced)
- 2 spring onions (minced)
- Sauce:
- 4 Tbsp soy sauce
- 4 Tsp sugar
- (-2 Tsp corn starch)
- some water to thin it out

## Steps
1. Eggplants into big chunks.
2. Add a generous amount of salt and let it sit for 10mins.
3. Drain, wash and pat dry.
4. Fry in oil until they become soft and charred, then remove from the pan.
5. Fry garlic, ginger and onion for 2mins before adding the eggplant again.
6. Add the sauce
7. Stir and cooked 1-2mins until thickened.

## Ingredients
- 1 crown of broccoli, cut into florets
- 500g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into large chunks
- Cornstarch
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, minced
- Zest of 1 orange
- Juice of 1 large orange
- 1/4 cup soy sauce, to taste
- Rice vinegar, to taste (approximately 2 tbsp)
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar

## Steps
1. Steam/Boil Broccoli until just fork-tender.
2. Move the broccoli to cold water to stop cooking.
3. Season the chicken chunks with salt and cornstarch until they are not wet or sticky.
4. Cook chicken until crusts are formed.
5. Mince the garlic and ginger.
6. Zest the orange and set aside.
7. Once the chicken is half cooked, add the minced garlic and ginger. Stir-fry for about a minute.
8. Deglaze the pan with soy sauce, rice vinegar, and orange juice. Add the granulated sugar and stir well.
9. Return the broccoli to the pan and stir to combine. The moisture from the broccoli will help prevent the sauce from burning.
10. Soy sauce and vinegar to taste.
11. Scatter a bit of cornstarch over the dish and stir aggressively to thicken the sauce.
12. Add orange zest, then turn off the heat.
13. Give the dish a final stir after cleaning up to prevent the zest from becoming bitter.

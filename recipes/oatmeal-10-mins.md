## Ingredients
- 0,7 cup oat
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking powerder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 over ripe bananas
- 1 ½ cups almond milk
- 2 big tbs creamy_peanut butter
- 1 tbs maple syrup
- 1 tsp chia seed
- ½ tsp vanilla extract

## Steps
1. Oven 190C
2. Baking dish: Oats, cinnamon, baking powder and salt.
3. Mixing bowl: mashed banana, almond milk, peanut butter, maple syrup, flaxseed and vanilla extract.
4. 5 minutes for the flaxseed to set.
5. Mixing bowl -> baking dish.
6. ﻿﻿﻿Bake uncovered until the top of the oatmeal is golden and the mixture is set, about 30-35 minutes.
7. Cool for 5 minutes.

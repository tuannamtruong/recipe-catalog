## Ingredients
- 1 cup diced cucumber
- 1 cup shelled edamame
- 3 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp cane sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp chili oil
- 1 garlic clove grated
- 1/4 cup diced green onion
- 1 tsp sesame seed

## Steps
1. Mix all together
2. In the fridge for 4 hours+

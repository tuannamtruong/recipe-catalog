## Ingredients
- 4 -5 large chicken breasts
- Salt and Pepper to taste
- all-purpose flour
- 6 Tbsp olive oil
- 300g fresh mushrooms
- 1 medium onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- Sauce:
- 3 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour for the sauce
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- ½ cup milk + ½ cup heavy cream

## Steps
1. Mushroom thickly sliced
2. Onion finely diced
3. Garlic minced
4. Chicken 1 inch thick strips. Season: pepper, salt.
5. Med high heat, brown both side, transfer to a baking dish.
6. Saute mushroom, onion until brown. Add garlic. Transfer to baking dish or a bowl.
7. Med low heat. Cook butter and flour for 2 mins. Add chicken broth, flour and lemon juice. Add milk and heavy cream.
8. Pour sauce over chicken in a pot/baking dish.
9. Bake 175C for 40 mins, or cook with stove.

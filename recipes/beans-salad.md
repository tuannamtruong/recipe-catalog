## Ingredients
- 2 cans of beans
- 1 can kirchenerbsen
- 1 can corn
- 1 bell pepper
- 2 lime/lemons
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 avocado
- 1 tbs Olive oil
- Basil

## Steps
1. Rinse the beans
2. Bean + erbsen + corn + pepper + tomatoes
3. Lime + olive oil + basil

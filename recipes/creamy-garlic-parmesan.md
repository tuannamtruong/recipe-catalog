## Ingredients
- 1 kg chicken breast.
- 2 tsp smoked paprika powder
- 2 tbsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 4 tbsp oil ( for marinade )
- 4 tbsp clarified butter/ghee
- 2 tbsp butter
- Sauce:
- 2 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 tbsp fresh garlic, minced
- 4 tbsp all purpose flour
- 700 ml chicken broth
- 700 ml heavy whipping cream
- Salt
- 2 tbs smoked paprika powder
- 2 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp dried parsley
- 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 200g Parmesan cheese, freshly grated

## Steps
1. Marinate chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika powder and oil.
2. Medium heat, add butter.
3. Add the chicken and brown both sides. Remove, slice and put aside .
4. Sauté onions with butter until translucent.
5. Add the garlic and sauté until fragrant.
6. Add the seasoning, salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked pepper powder, red chili flakes and Italian seasoning. Mix everything together and add the flour.
7. Stir in the flour and cook for one minute.
8. Gradually add the chicken broth and mix until the sauce thickens. Cook on medium heat for 2-4 minutes.
9. Stir in the heavy whipping cream and whisk until the sauce thickens further.
10. Add the freshly grated Parmesan cheese and parsley, mixing well.
11. Add in pasta and top it with sliced chicken.

## Ingredients
- 150g chicken drumsticks
- 250g chicken thighs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 60g unsalted butter
- Stew ingredients:
- 300g white mushrooms, halved if small
- 2 medium brown onions, same as mushroom
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) white wine
- 3 cups (720 ml) chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) heavy cream

## Steps
1. Season chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Medium-high heat. Melt butter. Brown chicken thighs both sides.
3. Then brown the drumsticks.
4. Then remove from skillet.
5. Add mushrooms, onion, bay leave and thyme. Cook until mushroom is lightly golden.
6. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
7. Add flour and cook for 1 minute.
8. Add wine, chicken stock, salt and pepper.
9. Return chicken back into the sauce.
10. Simmer covered 10 minutes. Adjust heat so it's bubbling constantly but not rapidly.
11. Remove lid and let it simmer for a further 20 minutes.
12. Remove chicken to a plate. Add cream and stir.

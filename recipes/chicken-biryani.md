## Ingredients
- 1kg chicken thighs
- Marinade:
- 200 ml plain yogurt
- 180 ml water
- 3 tbs plain oil
- 9 minced garlic
- 2 tsp grated ginger
- 1/6 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/3 tsp cinnamon
- 2/3 tsp cayenne
- 2/3 tsp ground cardamom
- 3 tsp garam marsala
- 3 tsp coriander
- 1,5 tbs cumin
- 3 tbs paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- Rice:
- 3 tbsp salt
- 12 cloves
- 6 bay leaves
- 1 star anise
- 8 green cardamon pods
- 600g uncooked rice
- Main:
- 1,3 cup (312 ml) coriander / cilantro, chopped
- 80g ghee or unsalted butter
- 3 medium onions, thinly sliced
- Yoghurt

## Steps
1. >Marinade< in a large pot with chicken and coat well. 20 minutes to overnight.
2. Make the >Rice<.
3. Caramelize the onions for garnish.
4. Medium heat.
5. Cover and cook Marinaded pot for 5 minutes.
6. Remove lid. Cook for 5 minutes, turning chicken twice.
7. Remove from heat.
8. Turn chicken so skin side is down – it should cover most of the base of the pot.
9. Scatter over half the onion then half the coriander.
10. Top with all the rice. Gently pat down and flatten surface.
11. Drizzle over ghee/butter.
12. Place lid on. Return to stove over medium heat.
13. As soon as it's steaming, turn down to low then cook for 25 minutes.
14. Remove from stove, rest with lid on for 10 minutes.
15. Garnish with remaining onion and coriander with yoghurt on the side

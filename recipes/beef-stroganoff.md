## Ingredients
- 500g sirloin steak
- salt
- pepper
- 1 tbs olive oil
- 3 tbs butter
- 1 cup diced onion
- 250g mushrooms, halved
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- ⅓ cup brandy, cognac, (wine)
- 1½ cups beef stock
- 1 tbs soy sauce
- 1½ tsp dijon mustard
- 3 Tablespoons flour
- ½ cup sour cream, crème fraîche, or Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup chopped parsley

## Steps
1. Cut the sirloin steak into thin strips and season it with salt and pepper.
2. Oil and 1 tbs butter in skillet medium high heat. Cook strips in single layer. Move strips into a bowl.
3. 2 tbs butter to skillet. Add the onions and sauté.
4. Add mushrooms with salt, and cook until nicely browned.
5. Add garlic and cook for 1 min.
6. Deglaze with alcohol. Cook for 2 to 4 minutes until the liquid has reduced by about a third.
7. In a large bowl: beef broth, soy sauce, mustard, and flour until smooth.
8. Beef broth mixture to skillet, and stir. Simmer and cook for 5 mins. Stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened slightly.
9. Turn the heat to low, and stir in the sour cream.
10. Return the cooked steak to the pan and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, or until the steak is heated through.

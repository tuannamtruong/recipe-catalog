## Ingredients
- 3 chicken thighs
- Cornstarch
- Cajun cream sauce:
- 2 tbsp of butter
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- ½ cup of chicken broth
- ½ cup of heavy cream
- 1 cup of parmesan cheese
- ½ lemon juice
- A handful of spinach
- Seasoning for the sauce:
- 2 tbs of paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp onion powerder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp of chili flakes
- 2 tsp of blend of dried herbs (oregna, thyme, basil…)

## Steps
1. Chicken + salt + pepper + cornstarch.
2. Medium-high heat. Golden both side. Set aside.
3. butter + onion +garlic. Sauté.
4. Add in Cajun seasoning, paprika, chili flakes and Italian seasoning. Sauté for 30 seconds or until it is fragrant.
5. Add in chicken broth, heavy cream and stir until the sauce thickens.
6. Add in parmesan cheese, squeeze ½ lemon and give it a good mix until the cheese dissolves.
7. Add back the chicken and baste the chicken with the sauce.

## Ingredients
- Main:
- 1 kg chicken
- 200 g onions chopped finely
- 1 tsp cumin seeds/jeera
- 2 Kashmiri dried red chili
- 1,5 tbs ginger garlic paste
- 600 g tomato puree
- Veggies:
- 1 cup (240 ml) red onion
- 3 bell peppers
- Spices:
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- ½  tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 2,5 tbs >Kadai masala<
- Kadai Masala:
- 1,5 tbs coriander seed
- 1 tsp fennel (sub: cumin)
- 2 cloves (sub: 1/8 tsp nutmeg)
- 1/2 inch cinnamon (1/4 tsp)
- 3 green cardamoms (1/2 tsp)
- 3 black pepper corn (sub: 1/8 tsp crushed)
- 1 dried red chili (1/4 tsp)
- Sub for 1 tbs Kadai Masala = 1,5 tsp coriander powder + 1/4 cumin + 1/2 chili powder + 3/4 tsp garam masala.
- Cashew puree:
- 3 tbs heavy cream / yogurt
- 10 cashews, powdered
- 4 tbs water

## Steps
1. Chop the onion finely.
2. Med heat. 2,5 tbs oil/ghee.
3. Add cumin and chili, cook until fragrant.
4. Add onions and saute until golden. Makes Kadai Masala if needed.
5. Stir in garlic paste for 30-60s.
6. Add chicken. Saute until pale.
7. Med-low heat. Stir in the >Spices<
8. Pour tomatoes puree in and mix well. Med heat and cook until thicken, 3-4 mins.
9. Add >Veggies< + 100ml water or stir fry them separately for 2-3 mins.
10. Glaze with >Cashews puree<

## Ingredients
- Gạo
- Gừng
- Thịt (gà)

## Steps
1. Nấu cơm trong nồi tầm 1h. Gạo : nước 5.
2. Luộc gà tầm 20-30 phút. Đến khi nhừ.
3. Vớt gà. Xé gà. Gà, nước gà và gừng vào cháo. Đun thêm tầm 30 phút.
4. Drinks
5. Try:
6. 50 aperol 30 limon 20 zucker syrup
7. https://www.instagram.com/p/Czt_R0Xqqo7/
8. 15 mins
9. 1 cup (250ml) water
10. 1 knob ginger, smashed or grated
11. 1 stick of cinnamon
12. 4 (4/6 tsp) cardamom pods
13. 3 peppercorns
14. 2 black tea bags
15. 1 cup (250ml) milk of your choice
16. 20g dark chocolate
17. 1 Tbsp cacao powder
18. First 5 ingredients
19. Either lightly smash first or immediately put in hot water.
20. Simmer for 2mins.
21. Add tea bags
22. Simmer for 2mins.
23. Add milk, chocolate, cacao powder.
24. Bring to a boil twice, then strain and serve warm.
25. Either lightly smash first or immediately put in hot water.
26. Simmer for 2mins.
27. Simmer for 2mins.
28. Bring to a boil twice, then strain and serve warm.

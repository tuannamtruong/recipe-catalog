## Ingredients
- 1 cup (240 ml) fresh orange juice
- zest of 1 orange
- ⅓ cup (115 g) honey
- 4 tbs soy sauce
- 1 tbs minced garlic
- 1 tbs rice vinegar
- 2 tbs brown sugar
- 1 tbs chili garlic sauce
- orange zest
- 1 tbs cornstarch + 2 tbs water slurry

## Steps
1. Mix all together in med-high heat
2. Cook until thicken.

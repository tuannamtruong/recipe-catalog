## Ingredients
- 3 chicken breasts (halves)
- Flour
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons butter divided
- 1 whole head garlic cloves peeled
- 1/2 cup chicken broth or stock
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 cup heavy/whipping cream
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Steps
1. Season: salt, pepper and flour.
2. Skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Olive oil and 1 tbsp of the butter. Brown the chicken, then take the chicken and set it aside.
4. Heat to medium, add the rest of the butter to the pan. Let it melt then add the garlic cloves. Cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring often, until they're lightly browned on the outside.
5. Add the chicken broth, lemon juice, and garlic powder to the pan. Let it bubble for around 4 minutes or until the liquid is noticeably reduced.
6. Stir in the cream and add the chicken back into the pan. Cook until the sauce is reduced. Serve with freshly chopped parsley if you wish.

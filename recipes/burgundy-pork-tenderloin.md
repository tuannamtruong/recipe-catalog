## Ingredients
- 500g pounds pork tenderloin
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 1 stalk celery/kohlrabi
- 2 cups red wine/red grapes juice
- 3 tbs (rind) gravy

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 175C.
2. Salt, pepper, garlic powder to meat.
3. Brown the outside of the meat for 2 mins.
4. Place pork in a baking dish with onion and celery, and pour wine over all.
5. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes.
6. When done baking, remove meat from baking dish.
7. Pour gravy mix into baking dish. Stir until thickened.

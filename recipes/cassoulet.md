## Ingredients
- 200g white beans
- Some slices of fatty pork ham (diced)
- 200g sausage
- 500g chicken leg
- 1-2 carrots (diced)
- 1 onion (diced)ss
- 1-2 celeries (diced)
- 4 Garlics (diced)
- 1tbs tomatoes paste or any tomaty
- Any 400ml stock
- Any herbs (rosmary, 2 bay leaf or green herbs)

## Steps
1. White beans soaked in salt for 2 hours
2. Brown ham with no oil
3. Add sausage and butter (if not enough fat)
4. Add chicken legg
5. Add 2 carrots, 1 onion, 1 celery
6. 4 Garlics
7. Add stock
8. Add herbs
9. Cook for 60 – 90 mins
10. HACKFLEISCH
11. Thịt băm + nấm + tỏi tiêu đường + hành
12. Thịt băm + ngô + trứng + cà chua + hành

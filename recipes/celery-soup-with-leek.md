## Ingredients
- 2 celeries (250g)
- 150g Butter
- 1 white part of leek
- 250g celeriac
- 1 onion
- (5 garlics cloves)
- 1 small-medium size potato (g?)
- Chicken/veggie broth (200ml?)
- 500ml Sour/ heavy cream
- (Milk)
- Celeries leafs for garnish

## Steps
1. Everything are finely chopped
2. Medium heat, butter.
3. Add onion, celeries, leek ,celeriac.
4. Cook softly(10-15 mins).
5. Different pot, boil potatoes.
6. Add double cream to potatoes pot.
7. Add broth to potatoes pot.
8. When the veggies are soft and sweated, the potatoes cream are close to boiling. Add the veggies to potatoes. Set it to gentle heat. Cook for 10 mins.
9. But everything to blender and hit it. Around 1 min, has to be soupy texture.
10. Add celeries leaf for garnish.

## Ingredients
- 700g chicken
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 large yellow onion
- 2 tbsp Olive oil
- 2 tbs butter
- powder:
- 3 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 tsp sweet paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- wet:
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups (480ml) beef stock
- creams:
- 2 tbs sour cream
- 2 tbs whipping cream

## Steps
1. brown chicken - remove
2. med heat - butter cook onion till not hard
3. flour in  - cook onion till soft
4. powder in for 1 min
5. wet for 1 min
6. chicken back + reduce heat to low and simmer for 10 mins
7. add creams to finish

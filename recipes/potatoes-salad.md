## Ingredients
- 1kg Potatoes
- Olive oil
- Sauce:
- Chili powder, cumin
- Tomato & paprika paste
- Pomegranate molasses (subs: 1 tbs Honey and 1 tsp lemon juice)
- Ground red pepper
- Veggies:
- Bell pepper
- Red onions
- Parsley
- Spring onions
- Kicker:
- Pecan
- Pickles

## Steps
1. Preheat oven 220C.
2. Slice potatoes, generous olive oil, salt and pepper. Bake for 40 mins.
3. Increase to 240C, remove the potatoes and mix around. Then bake for 10-15 mins for crispiness.
4. Mix >Sauce< together.
5. Sliced >Veggies< thinly and add >Kicker<.
6. Mix well with >Sauce<.
7. Add Potatoes and mix again.

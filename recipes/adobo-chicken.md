## Ingredients
- Main mix:
- 4 chicken thighs (1kg)
- 8 tbs white wine
- 8 tbs soy sauce
- 8 tbs brown sugar
- 4 cloves garlic whole
- 4 phalanx ginger
- 3 green onions
- 4 tsp black pepper
- Optional
- 2 bay leaf
- Cinanmon stick
- 1 onion, quartered

## Steps
1. Marinate Main mix overnight.
2. Water enough to flood everything
3. Bring to a boil then simmer 20 mins

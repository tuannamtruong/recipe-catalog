## Ingredients
- 400g tofu
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 spring onion
- 1 Tbsp chili flakes
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1/4 cup (60ml) soy sauce
- 1/4 cup (60ml) water
- 2 Tbsp syrup or sugar

## Steps
1. Pat tofu dry, cut in thick slices and fry in oil on both sides.
2. Onion and garlic, finely chop.
3. Mix spices and add the sauce to the tofu.
4. Let it simmer for 5-10mins.

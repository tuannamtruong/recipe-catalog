## Ingredients
- Salad:
- 2 cucumber
- 6 cherry tomatoes
- 2 red onion
- 400 g sweet corns
- spring onion
- 400 g can tuna
- something spicy (jalapeno, chili flakes)
- salt n pepper
- Tortilla
- Sauce:
- mayo
- greek yogurt
- hot sauce

## Steps
1. cubes, slices everything into a bowl with spices
2. brown tortilla
3. makes sauce on the side

## Ingredients
- 2 eggplants
- Cut in chunks.
- a good drizzle of (olive) oil
- salt to taste
- 1 Tsp curry powder

## Steps
1. Mix and place on a baking tray.
2. Bake at 200C/400F for 25-30mins.
3. Serve with sriracha Mayo or your favorite sauce.

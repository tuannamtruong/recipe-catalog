## Ingredients
- 500g chicken thighs
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ cup unsalted butter
- ¼ cup hot sauce
- 3 tablespoons water
- ⅓ cup finely chopped celery
- + try other thing

## Steps
1. Heat a dry saucepan over high heat. Place thighs skin-side down in the hot pan and cook until skin is browned, seasoning generously with salt, about 5 minutes.
2. Turn thighs over once skin no longer sticks to the pan. Add butter, Louisiana hot sauce, and water. Toss thighs around to stir the sauce until butter is melted.
3. Heat to low, cover, and simmer until meat can be easily pulled from the bone, about 1 1/2 hours. Remove from heat and break meat up into smaller pieces using tongs, stirring it into the sauce. Let mixture cool to room temperature, at least 20 minutes.
4. Bring the sauce in the pan to a boil over medium-high heat. reduce until slightly thickened, about 2 minutes. Pour sauce into the bowl of chicken meat and mix thoroughly. Taste for seasoning; add salt, cayenne, and hot sauce as needed. Add celery and mix one last time.

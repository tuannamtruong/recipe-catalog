## Ingredients
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1000 ml chickpeas
- 15 sliced thin garlic
- 1/4 cups fresh oregano (1 tbs dried)
- 1 tbs honey
- 2 strips of lemon peel
- dash of red chilli flakes
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- (240 ml feta cut into 4 thinner blocks)
- garnish with fresh oregano or basil

## Steps
1. All together in oven for 25 mins 175C
2. (Add cheese block
3. Broil a little bit
4. Mix together again)
5. Garnish wit hot honey and greens

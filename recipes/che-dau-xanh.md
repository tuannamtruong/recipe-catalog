## Ingredients
- 300g đậu xanh bóc vỏ
- 100g sugar
- 200ml coconut milk
- 3 tbs Tapioca starch

## Steps
1. Soak the beans in water for 2 hours
2. In a pan, water 1 cm higher, cook for 20 mins
3. Add 0,5l water, cook for 15 mins
4. Add sugar
5. Starch with water, mix well, add to bowl. Turn to low heat.
6. Add coconut milk.

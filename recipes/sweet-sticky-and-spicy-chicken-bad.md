## Ingredients
- 1 chicken breast
- MIX:
- Brown sugar
- Honey
- Soy sauce
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Hot sauce

## Steps
1. Combine MIX.
2. Lightly salt and pepper the chicken strips.
3. Medium heat. Add chicken strips and brown on both sides.
4. Pour the sauce over the chicken. Simmer uncovered until the sauce thickens, 8 to 10 minutes.

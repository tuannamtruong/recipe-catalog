## Ingredients
- 1 small white onion (200 g) chopped
- 1 large red bell pepper (170 g) chopped
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning divided
- 500g ground turkey
- 425 g can black beans rinsed and drained
- 284 g can Rotel drained
- 113 g can diced green chiles
- 57 g reduced fat cream cheese
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) non fat plain greek yogurt
- 1 batch homemade enchilada sauce
- 5 extra thin corn tortillas
- 85 g colby jack cheese (84 g) shredded cilantro, limes, avocado, lettuce, sour cream optional, for topping

## Steps
1. Oil on medium high heat.
2. Chopped onion, pepper, and 1/2 tsp taco seasoning. Cook until veggies have softened- about 5 minutes.
3. Add ground meat to the onion and pepper, along with the other 1/2 tsp taco seasoning and a pinch of salt to taste. Cook meat, breaking apart into small pieces, until fully cooked - about another 5 minutes.
4. Add black beans, Rotel, green chiles, cream cheese, greek yogurt, and enchilada sauce, stirring everything together until fully combined.
5. Cut the tortillas into strips and stir in, making sure each piece gets coated in the sauce.
6. Top with shredded cheese and cover with a lid to melt.
7. Add cilantro, limes, any other toppings

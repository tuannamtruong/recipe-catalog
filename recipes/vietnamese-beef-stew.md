## Ingredients
- Beef Marinade:
- 1 kg beef goulash
- 1 tsp red curry powder
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tbs sugar
- 1 tbs chicken bouillon powder
- 2 tbs garlic (minced)
- 2 tbs shallots (minced)
- 4 large carrots
- 2 large lemongrass
- 2-inch knob ginger
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 200g tomato paste
- 400ml coconut juice
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 whole star anise
- 1 tbs fish sauce
- ½ tablespoon light soy sauce

## Steps
1. Marinate beef cube for at 30 minutes to overnight
2. Cut carrot into the same size as the beef.
3. Discard the lemongrass leafy tops, if any.
4. Cut the lemongrass stalk into 4" segments.
5. Slice the ginger into thick coins
6. Brown beef in med-high.
7. Clear the center.
8. Add the lemongrass and ginger.
9. Pan fry for about one minute.
10. Stir in tomato paste.
11. Add coconut soda, and water.
12. Braise with the lid on for 1-½ hours on very low heat.
13. Add carrots, cinnamon stick and whole star anise. Continue cooking with a covered lid on a low simmer for about 30 minutes.

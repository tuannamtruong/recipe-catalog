## Ingredients
- Soup:
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 454 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 cup (240 ml) red lentils, rinsed
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- Fresh Herbs:
- mixed parsley, dill, mint
- Topping:
- 1 cup (240 ml) plain full-fat yogurt
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- spice it up with some cumin, coriander, cinnamon, and red pepper flakes.

## Steps
1. Med heat with oil
2. Softend onion + garlic
3. Lentil, chicken breast, tumeric, salt, black pepper in, then soaks everything water 5cm over. Simmer for 2 hours.
4. Remove the chickens, then shredded.
5. Add the chicken back with the Herbs.
6. Add the Topping when serve.

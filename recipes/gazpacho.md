## Ingredients
- Main:
- 4 large fresh tomatoes, peeled and diced
- ½ cucumber, peeled and diced
- ½ cup finely diced red bell pepper
- ¼ cup minced green onion
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 pinch dried oregano
- Blender:
- 1/3 from Main
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 1 lime, juiced
- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbs balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbs thinly sliced fresh basil

## Steps
1. Combine Main in a large bowl.
2. Stir in 1 tsp salt and black pepper
3. Put Blender into the blender. Cover and purée until smooth.
4. Pour puréed mixture through a strainer into the tomato-cucumber mixture; stir to combine.
5. Place 1/3 of the tomato mixture into the blender. Cover, turn blender on, and purée until smooth. Return puréed mixture to the remaining tomato-cucumber mixture. Stir to combine. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for 2 hours.
6. (Season cold soup with salt and black pepper to taste. Ladle into bowls and top with basil.)

## Ingredients
- (rice)
- Salad:
- 1 - 2 large cucumbers thinly sliced 1 cup edamame
- 1 bunch green onions thinly sliced 1 avocado chopped
- Crispy chicken Tenders
- Chopped peanuts
- (Sweet Chilli Creamy Dressing)
- 1/4 cup Greek yoghurt
- 1/4 cup mayo (I used light)
- 1/2 tsp paprika Water to thin

## Steps
1. Cook the chicken as instructed.
2. Add cucumber, edamame, green onion, avocado, cooked chicken and (rice) to a large bowl.
3. Make the dressing by whisking all ingredients until smooth using water to thin.
4. Pour dressing over the salad and toss gently.
5. Garnish with sesame seeds or peanuts.

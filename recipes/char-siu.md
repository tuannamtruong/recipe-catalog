## Ingredients
- 600g pork butt or fatty cut. Fat is important.
- 4 tbs combination of (oyster + soy) sauce
- 3 tbs combination of (hoisin + red wine) sauce
- 4 tbs honey
- 3 tbs brown sugar
- 2 tbs chinese rice wine (sake, dry sherry)
- 4 tbs ketchup
- 4  garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp five-spice powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 thumb size giner

## Steps
1. All sauce together.
2. Cut the meat to proper size if needed.
3. Poke the meat and marinate 4-12 hours.
4. Before cook room temperature.
5. Preheat 150C
6. In oven 60 mins.
7. Brush marinate and flip.
8. In oven 30 mins.

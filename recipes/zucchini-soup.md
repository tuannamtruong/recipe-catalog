## Ingredients
- 1 tbs unsalted butter
- 2 tbs extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 5 garlic clove, thinly sliced
- 700g zucchini, halved lengthwise and sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 2/3 cup vegetable stock or low-sodium broth

## Steps
1. Moderately low heat, melt the butter in the olive oil.
2. Add the onion and garlic, season with salt and pepper, and, stirring frequently, until softened, 7 to 8 minutes.
3. Add the zucchini until softened, about 10 minutes.
4. Add the stock and 1 1/2 cups of water and bring to a simmer; cook until the zucchini is very soft, about 10 minutes.
5. Puree the soup in a blender.

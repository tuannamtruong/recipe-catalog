## Ingredients
- 600g chicken legs
- ¼ cup butter, cubed
- Chicken broth
- hot red pepper sauce
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- salt and pepper to taste
- or any powder

## Steps
1. Drumsticks in a oven or slow cooker.
2. All ingredients sprinkle evenly with pieces of butter.
3. Oven: 180C for 45 mins or until tender.
4. Fish
5. Bake fish in oven 220C 15 mins.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp rapeseed or sunflower oil
- 1 medium shallot, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, crushed or finely grated
- 1 tbsp black or green peppercorns
- 2 tbsp brandy
- 500g Beef Stock
- 100ml whipping cream
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard

## Steps
1. Med low heat
2. Shallot in the pan with pinch of salt
3. Shortly before any liquid, add garlic and peppercorn for 1 min.
4. Add brandy and stock
5. Boil and reduce it down.
6. Put in a fridge or keep cooking
7. Lower heat
8. Add whipping cream and mustard
9. Cook until thick enough

## Ingredients
- 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil
- 10 garlic cloves, grated
- 170g tomato paste
- 500g grounded beef
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp Black pepper
- 1 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 425g canned lentils
- 425g canned cannellini beans in vodka sauce
- If you can't find these, use a plain can of cannelini beans + 1 cup store bought vodka sauce + 1 Tsp olive oil
- 450ml beef broth
- 336ml water
- 336g pasta
- 120g Greek yogurt/Milk
- 30g parmesam

## Steps
1. Garlic + tomatoes paste some mins.
2. Beef in until brown.
3. 1 tsp salt
4. 1 tsp Black pepper
5. 1 tsp red chili flakes
6. 1 tsp Italian seasoning
7. 425g canned lentils
8. 425g canned cannellini beans in vodka sauce
9. 450ml beef broth
10. 336ml water
11. 336g pasta
12. Cover lid, cook for 20 mins. Until thick and creamy.
13. For finish, cilantro, milk/yogurt, parmesam
14. Wraps

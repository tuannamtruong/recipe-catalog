## Ingredients
- Pork loin
- Garlic
- Rosmarin
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil
- White wine

## Steps
1. Preheat oven 175C
2. Crush garlic, rosmary, salt and pepper together. Make a paste.
3. Pierce meat and press paste in. Rub remainder with olive oil outside.
4. Bake 1 hour.
5. White wine the burn stuff.

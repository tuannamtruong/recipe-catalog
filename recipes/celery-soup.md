## Ingredients
- 2 celeries (400g), thinly sliced
- 100g Butter or olive oil
- 1 white part of leek
- 250g celeriac
- 1 onion, diced
- 5 garlics cloves, chopped
- 300g potatoes, 1,5cm sliced
- 4 cups water + 1 tbs Chicken or veggie broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 2,2g dill
- 12g parsley
- 115g Sour cream
- Celeries leafs for garnish

## Steps
1. Oil/butter over medium heat
2. Add onion, stirring occasionally, letting the onions get soft golden, about 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic and stir 1-2 minutes, until fragrant.
4. Add celery, potatoes, broth, bay leaf, salt, pepper. The liquid should just cover the veggies.
5. Cover, bring to a boil, turn heat down and simmer gently until potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes.
6. Turn heat off, remove bay leaf
7. Add the herbs and cream
8. Transfer to a blender. Blend well
9. Add celeries leaf for garnish.

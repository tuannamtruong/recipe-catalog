## Ingredients
- 200g sirloin steak
- 3 shallots, peeled
- 200g mushrooms
- 1 tbs salted butter
- 1 garlic clove, peeled
- 1 -2 tbs Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbs Dijon mustard
- 1/3 cup brandy or cognac
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Small handful of flat-leaf parsley

## Steps
1. Roll flat the steak and season.
2. Brown both side in med high heat. Then set  aside.
3. Add shallot and season. Cook to soft.
4. Add mushroom, butter, and crushed garlic.
5. Add wochester sauce and mustard.
6. Add alcohol.
7. When alcohol is burnt off, add heavy cream and parsley.

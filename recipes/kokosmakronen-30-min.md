## Ingredients
- 140 g	Kokosraspel
- 4	Eiweiß
- 200 g	Zucker, feiner
- 80	Oblaten

## Steps
1. Sugar, Coconus and Eggwhite together in a pot together.
2. Warm it up to 50C. Not higher.
3. Making a mass on top of the Oblaten (with 2 tea spoons).
4. A bit water on top of backpaper will make the Oblate to not move so much.
5. Backofen bei 150°C Ober-/Unterhitze ca. 20 Minuten backen.

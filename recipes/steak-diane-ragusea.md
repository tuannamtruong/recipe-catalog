## Ingredients
- 200g sirloin steak
- 1 onion
- 200g mushrooms, minced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 -2 tbs Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbs (Dijon) mustard
- 1 tbs tomato paste
- 125ml heavy cream
- 1 cup rind broth
- (cognac or brown liquor)
- Lemon juice
- (Small handful of flat-leaf parsley)

## Steps
1. Season with salt, pepper and oil.
2. Brown both side in med high heat. Meat should be a bit red inside. Then set aside.
3. Add mushroom. When mushroom’s color turns brown, add onion, garlic and tomato paste.
4. When everything in pan is brown, deglaze with broth or liquor.
5. When the pan is dry down, add broth, mustard, Worchestershire sauce.
6. Cook until the liquid reduces down to a good amount. Add the meat back, crème and lemon juice until the sauce thicken.

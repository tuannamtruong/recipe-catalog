## Ingredients
- 1kg chicken thigh fillet
- 80gr butter
- 2,5 tbsp flour
- 700ml milk
- 3 lemon juice
- 3 tbsp honey
- Chicken bouillon powder
- MSG

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Medium heat. Brown chicken both sides.
3. Remove.
4. Make a roux.
5. Slowly pour in the milk while stirring continuously.
6. Add lemon juice + season to taste.
7. Return the chicken to the sauce and let it simmer for 1-2.

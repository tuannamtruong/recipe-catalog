## Ingredients
- Makes about 3/2 cup Peanut Dipping Sauce:
- 3/4 cup smooth all-natural, pure peanut butter
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 165ml coconut milch

## Steps
1. Mix erverything together, except coconut milk.
2. Cook cmilk in med heat, when hot move to the mixture.

## Ingredients
- For the Chicken:
- 700g chicken thighs/breast
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 2 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp allspice
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 8 garlic cloves
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 2 tsp chicken boulion
- For the Rice:
- 3 tbsp butter + 1 ½ cups yellow onion, diced
- 1 tbsp turmeric, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp pepper
- 1 ½ cups rice
- 3 cups hot water
- 3 tsp chicken boulion
- Garlic Sauce:
- ¼ cup mayonnaise + ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 to 3 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp fresh parsley
- 1 tsp salt + 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ lemon, juiced + 1 tbsp olive oil

## Steps
1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with the spices, olive oil, garlic, lemon juice and chicken boulion.
2. Marinate.
3. medium-high heat, olive oil.
4. Cook until golden and cooked through.
5. Set aside.
6. medium-low.
7. Add butter to the same pan then the onions, stirring often, until translucent.
8. Add the garlic & spices, cook for 1 to 2 mins.
9. Add the rice and toast for 2 mins while stirring.
10. Pour in the hot water mixed with chicken boulion. Stir & bring to a boil.
11. Once boiling, cover & reduce heat to low.
12. Simmer for 20 mins until cooked.

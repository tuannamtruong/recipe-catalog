## Ingredients
- 500g pork chop
- Salt and black pepper
- Dried:
- Ginger, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- Hot pepper
- (Pepper corn)
- Cumin or staronni
- Wet:
- Honey
- Soy sauce
- Hot sauce
- Vinegar or Ketchup
- (Rice Wine or Dried Cherry)
- Rind or Veg Broth

## Steps
1. Oven to 185C.
2. Salt and pepper. Med high heat, brown both side.
3. Dried spices in. 1 min.
4. Wet ingredients in.
5. Stock in, barely cover ribs.
6. In oven 30 mins. Turn and cook for another 30 mins.
7. Chicken - Generic

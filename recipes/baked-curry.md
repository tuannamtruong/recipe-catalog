## Ingredients
- Any group of vegtables for baking (Kohlrabi, Kurbis, Zucchini, Daikon…)
- 3 carrots
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- a thumbsize piece of ginger
- 1 can (400g) chopped tomatoes
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
- 200ml water
- 1 can (220g) chickpeas (+ some green peas if you want)
- 1 Tsp coriander, cumin, garam masala, paprika, turmeric
- salt to taste

## Steps
1. Chop veggies.
2. Mix everything.
3. Bake at 200C for 45mins.
4. Garnish with cilantro and lime juice

## Ingredients
- RIBS:
- 500g back pork ribs
- MIX:
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tbs cumin
- 1 tsp chili
- (2 tbs olive oil)
- (1/2 tbs sugar)
- SAUCE:
- (150ml) barbecue sauce
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tbs olive oil
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- (1 tsp dry mustard)
- (1 tsp hot pepper)

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to (130-150C).
2. (Make hole backside of rib)
3. Combine MIX. Season both side. Cover tray with foil and bake for 2-3 hours.
4. During the last 5 minutes of cook time, mix SAUCE ingredients.
5. Remove ribs from the oven, remove foil and spread the tops of the ribs with the barbecue sauce mixture.
6. Increase oven temperature to 200-220°C. Return ribs to the oven, uncovered, and bake for a further 10 minutes. Oven settings: broil or grill on medium-high heat for about 3 minutes.
7. Rest for 10 minutes
